Product 01 — Carer Scribe

NDIS compliance documentation. Done by AI. In any language.

Built for disability support businesses across Australia. Support workers document in their own language — Carer Scribe writes the compliant note. Coordinators get real-time visibility across the whole team.

A parent + child account structure. The business owns the data. Workers get access to only what they need.

  • 🌐
    Multilingual AI Notes

    Voice or text input in 11 languages: English, Hindi, Tagalog, Nepali, Arabic, Swahili, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Punjabi, Somali, Amharic — output always in compliant English.

    Live Compliance Score

    Every note gets scored 0–100% against the NDIS Practice Standards. Red, amber, green. Coordinators see the whole team's score at a glance.

    12-Rule NDIS Compliance Engine

    Credential Wallet

  • 📋
    Coordinator Dashboard

    One view of every note, every worker, every score, and every pending credential across the entire organisation.

Product 02 — Tax Exposure Platform

Know your ATO exposure before it becomes a debt.

Australia's NDIS workforce is one of the fastest-growing gig economy segments — and one of the most undertaxed. Our platform helps unregistered NDIS workers, ABN holders, and disability support businesses instantly assess their tax obligations, superannuation exposure, and GST position.

Try the Tool
🧑‍💼

Independent NDIS Workers

Unregistered or ABN support workers who aren't sure if they owe income tax, need to register for GST, or are missing superannuation contributions.

🏢

NDIS Support Businesses

Small businesses paying workers as contractors without realising they may have PAYG withholding, superannuation guarantee, or payroll tax obligations.

📋

Platform Workers (Mable, Kynd)

Gig workers on disability platforms who earn above the GST threshold or don't realise they've become deemed employees for tax purposes.

What the platform analyses

💰
Income Tax Exposure

Based on annual earnings, calculates likely income tax liability including Medicare Levy.

🏦
Superannuation Guarantee

Determines whether the business hiring you is legally required to pay superannuation on your behalf — even if you hold an ABN.

🧾
GST Registration Threshold

Checks whether your gross income exceeds $75,000 AUD and flags GST registration obligations.

📊
PAYG Withholding Risk

Assesses whether your working arrangements meet the ATO's definition of an employee vs genuine contractor.

🔒
Sham Contracting Exposure

Identifies red flags that could trigger an ATO sham contracting audit — for both workers and businesses.

📄
ATO Reporting Obligations

Maps your specific obligations: BAS lodgement, tax return deadlines, TPAR (Taxable Payments Annual Report) if applicable.
